✨ Season of Hope • Fayetteville, Georgia

Sharing Christmas Warmth with Children in Foster Care

Headquartered on Marquis Drive in Fayetteville, GA, Bloom Our Youth protects, supports, and empowers foster children and families across Georgia. Join our community this Christmas to ensure every child feels remembered, valued, and celebrated.

How You Can Help This Christmas

Your support directly serves foster youth in Fayette County and surrounding communities.

Bloom's Christmas Angel Tree

Sponsor a holiday gift for a child experiencing foster care. Unwrapped gifts labeled with the child's code can be dropped off at our Fayetteville headquarters.

Where to Find Bloom in Fayetteville, GA

Both of Bloom's buildings sit side by side on Marquis Drive—just off GA-85 (Glynn Street South) in Fayetteville, about 20 minutes southwest of Riverdale.

🎄 Main Office & Angel Tree Drop-Off

150 Marquis Drive, Fayetteville, GA 30214

Hours: Mon–Fri, 8:30 AM – 5:00 PM
Directions: Turn onto Marquis Drive off GA-85 South (near Grady Avenue). Look for Bloom Our Youth signage on your left.

🛍️ The Bloom Closet & Donation Center

115 Marquis Drive, Fayetteville, GA 30214

Hours: Tue–Sat, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
